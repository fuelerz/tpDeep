Our Company

Protective coatings, engineered around your application.

Tekno Paints has established itself as a leading manufacturer of protective coatings for chemical plants, marine applications, superstructures, dams and pipeline projects. With a vision, Tekno Paints started its journey in the year 2016 at Kundaim, and later on, it was shifted to Verna.

Today, Tekno Paints is engaged in the manufacturing of exclusive, high-performance specialty coatings — a wide range of Epoxy-based products, Poly-urathane coatings, and Alkyd-based industrial paints.
