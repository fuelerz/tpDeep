About Us

Specialty coatings, built on engineering.

Our Company

A specialist manufacturer of protective coatings.

The company is well equipped with technical staff and workmen who constantly develop new products and processes to suit specific applications and products in close interaction with clients.

We believe in designing customized products for customers as per their requirements which will serve the purpose of the end user.

From its humble origin, Tekno Paints has grown into a mini-giant in its own right and has earned recognition in the paint field. Today, Tekno Paints is engaged in the manufacturing of exclusive, high-performance specialty coatings. It has a plethora of Epoxy-based products, Poly-urathane coating and Alkyd-based industrial paints.
